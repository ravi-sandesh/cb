CHOKA BARAH RULES

🎯 Objective: Race all 4 pawns anti-clockwise around the outer ring, then spiral inward to the Center Home.

🐚 5-House (4 Shells): Mouths up = steps. All 4 up = Chowka (4 + EXTRA ROLL). All 4 down = Baara (8 + EXTRA ROLL).

🐚 7-House (6 Shells): Mouths up = steps. All 6 up = Chowka (6 + EXTRA ROLL). All 6 down = Baara (12 + EXTRA ROLL).

🔒 Cut Requirement: You MUST capture at least 1 opponent pawn before your pawns can enter the inner path toward Center Home.

✂️ Capturing: Land on a square with an opponent → they return to Home Base. You earn an EXTRA TURN!

🔗 Gatti (Doubles): If 2+ of YOUR pawns land on the same square, they form Gatti — they move together as one and CANNOT be captured by a single opponent pawn.

✖ Safe Squares ('X'): No captures on safe squares. All pawns coexist peacefully here.

🏠 Home Base Safety: Pawns in their own Home Base are completely safe — cannot be captured.

🎲 Extra Rolls: On Chowka/Baara or after a capture, you get an extra roll. You may move a DIFFERENT pawn each extra roll!
